# degree

degree of a vertex in a graph is the total number of edges incident to it or away from it (degree in or out)
in directed graph out degree of a vertex is total no. of outgoing vertex
and indegree is total no. of incoming edges

# trees

it is a connected graph with no cycle, if we remove all cycles from a graph, we got a tree

if we remove an edge from a tree, it no more remains a tree, it becomes a forest
